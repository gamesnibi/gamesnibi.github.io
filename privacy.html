Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 24, 2026 · Last updated: October 24, 2026

This privacy policy explains how Willow Pond Records (the "game" or the "app"), made by NiBi Games ("we", "us"), handles information. It applies to the game on Android and iOS and to this website.

The short version
  • We do not collect, store, share or sell any personal information.
  • There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking in the game.
  • Your progress is saved only on your own device. The game works without an internet connection.
  • If we ever add online features, ads or purchases, we'll update this policy before they go live.

1. Information we collect

None. Willow Pond Records does not collect or transmit personal information or personal data of any kind. In particular, the game does not collect:

  • your name, email address, phone number or any account details (the game has no accounts or sign-in);
  • your location, contacts, photos, files, camera or microphone;
  • advertising IDs, device identifiers or usage analytics;
  • payment information (the game has no purchases at this time).

The game does not request internet access on Android. All of its pictures, music and sounds are included in the app itself.

2. What the game saves on your device

To remember your progress, the game keeps a save file in the app's private storage on your device. It contains game information only, such as:

Saved on your deviceWhy
Coins, pearls, stars, upgrades and looks you ownSo you keep what you've earned
Fish you've caught, records found, lakes visited, boss attemptsYour Fish Guide, collection and progress
Fishing buddies you've unlocked and any names you give themSo your buddies stay how you set them up
Settings (music, sound and ambience volume, vibration, hints, radio station)Your preferences

This information never leaves your device through the game, and we cannot see it. If you rename a buddy, please don't type real personal information — but even if you do, it stays on your device.

Device backups. If you have turned on your phone's backup feature (for example Google backup on Android or iCloud backup on iPhone), your device may include the game's save in that backup. Backups are made by your device and stored in your own Google or Apple account under their privacy policies. We have no access to them.

3. Device permissions

Vibration (Android: VIBRATE)Haptic feedback — the reel's clicks and a buzz when a fish bites. You can turn it off in the game's Settings.

The game does not ask for any other permission.

4. Third parties

The game contains no third-party advertising, analytics, social or tracking software, and we do not share, sell or rent information with anyone.

You download the game through an app store (Google Play or the Apple App Store). Those stores handle your download, account and any reviews you write under their own privacy policies: Google Privacy Policy · Apple Privacy Policy. If you have agreed to share diagnostics with the store, it may give us anonymous, combined statistics such as crash rates or install counts; these do not identify you.

5. Children's privacy

Willow Pond Records is a family-friendly game suitable for a general audience. Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it does not collect personal information from children, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age where they live). If you believe a child has somehow sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Your choices and deleting your data

  • Erase your progress: open the game's Settings and choose Start over.
  • Remove everything: uninstall the game. This deletes its save from your device.
  • Vibration and sound: can be turned off in the game's Settings at any time.

Because we don't hold any of your data, there is nothing stored with us to request or delete — but you are always welcome to contact us with questions.

7. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, privacy laws such as Canada's PIPEDA, the EU and UK GDPR, or California's CCPA may give you rights to access, correct or delete personal information that a company holds about you, and to know whether it is sold or shared. We do not collect, sell or share personal information, so we hold none about you. If you have a question or want to exercise a right, email us and we will reply within 30 days.

8. Security

Your save stays in the game's private storage on your device, protected by your device's own security. Since we don't collect or transmit your information, it isn't stored on any of our servers.

9. This website

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and has no analytics or ads. Like any web host, GitHub may record technical information such as your IP address in server logs for security and reliability; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

10. Changes to this policy

We may add features in future updates — for example online leaderboards, clubs, community events, advertising or in-app purchases. If we do, we will update this policy before those features are released, explain exactly what information is used and why, and change the "Last updated" date above. Where the law requires it, we will ask for your consent first.
