This privacy policy explains how Pond Reels (the "game" or the "app"), made by NiBi Games ("we", "us"), handles information. It applies to the game on Android and iOS, to its watch app on Wear OS and to the NiBi Games website.

The short version
  • We do not collect, store, share or sell any personal information.
  • Right now there are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking in the game.
  • Your progress is saved only on your own device. The game works without an internet connection.
  • Optional reminders are scheduled on your phone itself; nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Optional rewarded ads and in-app purchases are coming in an update. Ads will always be your choice, and purchases go through your app store. Section 5 explains what they'll involve.

1. Information we collect

None. Pond Reels does not collect or transmit personal information or personal data of any kind. In particular, the game does not collect:

  • your name, email address, phone number or any account details (the game has no accounts or sign-in);
  • your location, contacts, photos, files, camera or microphone;
  • advertising IDs, device identifiers or usage analytics;
  • payment information (the game has no purchases at this time).

The game does not request internet access on Android. All of its pictures, music and sounds are included in the app itself.

2. What the game saves on your device

To remember your progress, the game keeps a save file in the app's private storage on your device. It contains game information only, such as:

Saved on your deviceWhy
Coins, pearls, upgrades, bait and shop decorations you ownSo you keep what you've earned
Fish you've caught, lake songs you've found or bought with coins, lakes opened, trophies, boss attemptsYour Fish Guide, collection and progress
Your van, its decorations and trophies, pets, and the lake projects you've builtSo your rooms and lakes stay the way you made them

The reminders waiting to be shown (their time and text)So the phone can show them, and put them back after a restart

This information never leaves your device through the game, and we cannot see it.

Device backups. If you have turned on your phone's backup feature (for example Google backup on Android or iCloud backup on iPhone), your device may include the game's save in that backup. Backups are made by your device and stored in your own Google or Apple account under their privacy policies. We have no access to them.

4. Third parties

The game contains no third-party advertising, analytics, social or tracking software, and we do not share, sell or rent information with anyone.

You download the game through an app store (Google Play or the Apple App Store). Those stores handle your download, account and any reviews you write under their own privacy policies: Google Privacy Policy · Apple Privacy Policy. If you have agreed to share diagnostics with the store, it may give us anonymous, combined statistics such as crash rates or install counts; these do not identify you.

5. Ads and purchases (coming soon)

These aren't in the game yet. This section explains how they'll work, so nothing comes as a surprise. When they go live we'll add a note at the top of this page.

Optional rewarded ads. You'll be able to choose to watch a short video ad for a bonus, such as double coins for a catch or an extra boss attempt. An ad only plays when you tap to watch one, and the game never shows ads on its own. The ads will be served by Google AdMob. When an ad is requested or shown, AdMob may collect and use:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android) or, only if you allow tracking when asked, the advertising identifier (iPhone);
  • your IP address and the rough location it suggests (for example your country or city);
  • information about your device and the app, such as model, operating system, language and app version;
  • how you interact with the ad (for example whether it was watched to the end), to give you the reward and to prevent fraud.

Google uses this to show the ad, measure it, prevent fraud and, where allowed, to personalise ads. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game will ask for your consent before ads can be personalised, using Google's consent message. You can change that choice in the game's Settings. Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads; iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking). See how Google uses information from apps that use its services. We don't receive any of this information ourselves, only anonymous, combined totals such as how many ads were watched.

In-app purchases. Some optional extras, such as a bigger tackle box for boss attempts, will be sold through Google Play on Android or the App Store on iPhone. The store handles the payment. We never see your name, email address or payment details. The store tells the game on your phone what you bought, and the game keeps that with your save on your device. Purchases are handled under the Google Privacy Policy or Apple Privacy Policy.

To show ads and check purchases, the game will need internet access (Android: INTERNET), and on Android the ad software will use the AD_ID permission to read the advertising ID. Everything else in the game will keep working offline.

6. Children's privacy

Pond Reels is made for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it does not collect personal information from children, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age where they live). If you believe a child has somehow sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your choices and deleting your data

  • Erase your progress: open the game's Settings and choose Start over.
  • Remove everything: uninstall the game. This deletes its save from your device.
  • Vibration and sound: can be turned off in the game's Settings at any time.
  • Reminders: turn off Bramble's reminders in the game's Settings, or turn off notifications for Pond Reels in your phone's settings.
  • Ads (once available): simply don't tap to watch one. You can also change personalised-ads consent in the game's Settings, and reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Because we don't hold any of your data, there is nothing stored with us to request or delete, but you are always welcome to contact us with questions.

8. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, privacy laws such as Canada's PIPEDA, the EU and UK GDPR, or California's CCPA may give you rights to access, correct or delete personal information that a company holds about you, and to know whether it is sold or shared. We do not collect, sell or share personal information, so we hold none about you. If you have a question or want to exercise a right, email us and we will reply within 30 days.

9. Security

Your save stays in the game's private storage on your device, protected by your device's own security. Since we don't collect or transmit your information, it isn't stored on any of our servers.

10. The NiBi Games website

The NiBi Games website (gamesnibi.github.io) is hosted by GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and has no analytics or ads. Like any web host, GitHub may record technical information such as your IP address in server logs for security and reliability; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

11. Pond Reels on your watch

Pond Reels also has a watch app for Galaxy Watch and other Wear OS 3 and later watches. It's a standalone mini-game, and it keeps its progress (your coins, the fish you've caught and the lake you've chosen) only on the watch.

Like the phone game, it has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it doesn't send anything anywhere. Its only permission is vibration, for the bezel clicks and the buzz when a fish bites (see section 3). Uninstalling it from your watch deletes its progress.

Watch backups. Like the phone game, the watch app allows Android backup. If your watch backs up apps to your Google account, that backup may include the watch game's save. See Device backups in section 2.
