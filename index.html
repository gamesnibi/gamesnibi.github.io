What's in the water

A reel you can feel

Turn the brass wheel with real haptic clicks. Every fish fights its own way — runs, dives, jumps and head-shakes.

Four lakes, 34 fish

Willow Pond, Golden Oasis, Icy North and Jade Garden — each with its own fish, music and scenery.

Legendary bosses

Old Mossback, the Infernal Fish, Frostfin and the Techno Fish. Watch for the warnings and keep your finger nimble.

Bramble's shop

Barnaby Bramble sells upgrades, lucky charms and shiny new looks — and has a tall tale for every one.

Fishing buddies

Bring your dog, your niece, Uncle Walt or a parrot in a captain's hat. They cheer every catch.

The dock radio

Tune in to each lake's station or play the vintage records you've fished up.
