Pond Reels

A cozy fishing game about a quiet dock, a brass reel and lakes full of fish.

Coming soon to Android

Cast from a sleepy dock, turn the brass reel and see what's biting. Every fish fights in its own way, every lake has its own songs for the radio to find or buy with coins, and something big lives out in the deep water.

Pop into Barnaby Bramble's tackle shop to upgrade your gear and make the place your own, save up Angler Club tickets to open new lakes, and tune the dock radio to your favourite station.

We've been building it one fish at a time, and we honestly can't wait for you to cast your first line.

  • Fish of every shape and size, from tiny minnows to real monsters
  • A brass reel with real haptic feedback
  • Legendary fish and bosses with tricks of their own
  • A dock radio with songs for every lake to find or buy with coins
  • Plays offline. No accounts, no tracking.
  • Optional reminders from Bramble, scheduled on your phone

Lockin-Inator

A study timer that locks your phone down, plus a shield against endless short videos.

Beta testers wanted

Set a timer and Lockin-Inator pins your phone to it, turns on Do Not Disturb, and nudges you right back if you wander off into a distracting app. When the timer's done, everything unlocks on its own.

The rest of the time, the Shield quietly closes those endless short-video feeds while your messages and regular videos keep working. Want to turn it off? You'll have to sit through a 60-second Urge-Breaker first.

We made it for anyone who's ever picked up their phone to check one thing and looked up an hour later.

  • Study Mode keeps your phone locked until the timer ends
  • Closes endless short-video feeds
  • Messages and regular videos keep working
  • Silences notifications while you study (alarms still ring)
  • A 60-second Urge-Breaker before you can turn protection off
  • Free 7-day trial, then $1 a month or $20 for life

Lockin-Inator beta, limited spots

Be one of the first to lock in.

We're looking for Android beta testers for Lockin-Inator, our study timer that locks your phone, with a Shield that closes endless short-video feeds. Test it for two weeks and get a free Lifetime unlock when it launches.

  1. Ask to join the beta group

    Sign in with the Google account that's on your Android phone, then tap Ask to join group. That's how Google Play knows it's you.

    Join the beta group

  2. Once we approve you, opt in on Google Play

    We pick testers as spots open up and email you when you're in. Then tap Become a tester and install Lockin-Inator from Google Play.

    Opt in on Google Play

  3. Unlock it and tell us what you think

    We'll send you an access code that unlocks everything. Keep the app installed for at least 14 days and send feedback to gamesnibi@gmail.com.

Android only, ages 13 and up. Asking to join shares your Google account email with us; we only use it to run the beta. See the privacy policy.
