Pond Reels

A cozy fishing game about a quiet dock, a brass reel and four lakes full of fish.

Coming soon to Android

Cast from a sleepy dock, turn a brass reel you can feel in your hands, and fish four beautiful lakes. Every fish fights in its own way, old records turn up in waterproof sleeves, and a legendary boss lurks in every lake.

Visit Barnaby Bramble's tackle shop to upgrade your gear and decorate the place, open new lakes with Angler Club tickets, and tune the dock radio to your favourite station.

Privacy policyFeedback

  • A tactile brass reel with real haptic feedback
  • Four boss fish with their own attacks
  • Legendary fish with moves of their own
  • A dock radio with lake stations and vintage records
  • Plays offline — no accounts, no tracking

Lockin-Inator

A study timer that locks your phone down, plus a shield that blocks Reels and Shorts.

Coming soon to Android

Set a study timer and Lockin-Inator pins your phone to it, silences notifications, and sends you straight back if you open TikTok, Instagram or YouTube. When the timer ends, everything unlocks on its own.

The rest of the time, the Reels & Shorts Shield quietly closes Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and Facebook Reels, while your DMs and regular videos keep working. Want to switch it off? You'll have to sit through a 60-second Urge-Breaker first.

Feedback
  • Study Mode locks your phone until the timer ends
  • Blocks Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and Facebook Reels
  • DMs and regular videos keep working
  • Silences notifications while you study (alarms still ring)
  • 60-second Urge-Breaker before protection can be turned off
  • Free 7-day trial, then $1 a month or $15 for life
