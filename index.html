Pond Reels

A cozy fishing game about a quiet dock, a brass reel and lakes full of fish.

Coming soon to Android
Reel Them All!

Cast from a sleepy dock, turn the brass reel and see what's biting. Every fish fights in its own way, old records turn up in waterproof sleeves, and something big lives out in the deep water.

Pop into Barnaby Bramble's tackle shop to upgrade your gear and make the place your own, save up Angler Club tickets to open new lakes, and tune the dock radio to your favourite station.

We've been building it one fish at a time, and we honestly can't wait for you to cast your first line.

Lockin-Inator

A study timer that locks your phone down, plus a shield against endless short videos.

Coming soon to Android

Set a timer and Lockin-Inator pins your phone to it, turns on Do Not Disturb, and nudges you right back if you wander off into a distracting app. When the timer's done, everything unlocks on its own.

The rest of the time, the Shield quietly closes those endless short-video feeds while your messages and regular videos keep working. Want to turn it off? You'll have to sit through a 60-second Urge-Breaker first.

We made it for anyone who's ever picked up their phone to check one thing and looked up an hour later.

Feedback
  • Study Mode keeps your phone locked until the timer ends
  • Closes endless short-video feeds
  • Messages and regular videos keep working
  • Silences notifications while you study (alarms still ring)
  • A 60-second Urge-Breaker before you can turn protection off
  • Free 7-day trial, then $1 a month or $20 for life
