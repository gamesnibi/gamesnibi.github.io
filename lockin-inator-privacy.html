Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026 · Last updated: October 27, 2026 (beta testing added)

This privacy policy explains how Lockin-Inator (the "app"), made by NiBi Games ("we", "us"), handles information. It applies to the Lockin-Inator app for Android.

The short version
  • The app does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information.
  • The Shield uses Android's Accessibility Service to spot Reels, Shorts and distracting apps. What it sees is checked on your phone, in the moment, and is never saved or sent anywhere.
  • There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Purchases go through Google Play. We never see your payment details.
  • If you ask to join the beta, we get your Google account email from Google Groups and use it only to run the test (section 7).

1. Information we collect

None. The Lockin-Inator app does not collect or send us personal information or personal data of any kind. (Joining the beta is separate; see section 7.) In particular, the app does not collect:

  • your name, email address, phone number or any account details (the app has no accounts or sign-in);
  • your messages, contacts, photos, videos, files, location, camera or microphone;
  • what you watch, read or type in other apps;
  • advertising IDs, device identifiers or usage analytics.

2. The Shield and the Accessibility Service

The Shield is Lockin-Inator's blocker. It uses Android's Accessibility Service, which lets an app see what's on screen inside other apps. Lockin-Inator only turns it on after you read and agree to an explanation in the app, and after you switch it on yourself in your phone's Accessibility settings.

What it looks atWhy
What's on screen inside Instagram, YouTube and Facebook (for example, which tab or screen you're on, and whether a reel in a chat was sent to you or by you)To recognise Reels and Shorts screens and close them, and to keep Instagram on its Messages tab if you choose that setting
Which app is open, during a study session only, for TikTok, Snapchat, Reddit, X, Pinterest, Twitch and Netflix (as well as the apps above)To send you back to Lockin-Inator while your study timer is running

All of this happens on your phone, as it happens. Lockin-Inator does not save, record or send anything the Shield sees, including your messages. The only thing it keeps is a count of how many times something was blocked, which is shown on the app's home screen.

The Shield acts only on the apps listed above. To close a Reels or Shorts screen it uses the same actions you could do yourself, such as going back. It never changes your settings, posts, sends or buys anything, or interacts with other people.

3. What the app saves on your phone

Lockin-Inator keeps a small settings file in its private storage on your phone:

Saved on your phoneWhy
Shield settings (which feeds to block, "Stay on Messages") and your colour themeYour preferences
The current study session's start and end time, and your last session lengthSo a session keeps running if the app is closed or the phone restarts
How many times the Shield blocked something, by appThe counts on the home screen
Whether you've agreed to the Shield's explanationSo the Shield only works after you agree
Whether you own Premium (monthly or Lifetime)So the app works offline

This information never leaves your phone through the app, and we cannot see it. The app also opts out of Android's backup, so none of it is copied to your Google account.

During a study session the app also uses Android's screen pinning, which you confirm on screen, to keep the timer in front.

5. Purchases

Lockin-Inator offers a free trial, a monthly subscription and a one-time Lifetime purchase. These are sold and processed by Google Play. We never receive your name, email address or payment details. Google Play tells the app on your phone whether you own Premium, and the app keeps that answer on your phone (section 3). You can cancel a subscription at any time in the Google Play Store under Payments & subscriptions. Google handles purchases under its own privacy policy.

6. Third parties

The app contains no third-party advertising, analytics, social or tracking software, and we do not share, sell or rent information with anyone.

You download the app and buy Premium through Google Play, which handles your download, account, purchases and any reviews you write under the Google Privacy Policy. If you have agreed to share diagnostics with Google Play, it may give us anonymous, combined statistics such as crash rates or install counts; these do not identify you.

7. Beta testing

If you ask to join the Lockin-Inator beta through our Google Group (lockin-inator-beta on Google Groups), Google shares your Google account name and email address with us as part of the join request. We use them only to run the beta: to approve you, add you to the test on Google Play, send you your access code and contact you about the test. We don't sell or share them, and we remove you from the group when the beta ends or whenever you ask. Google Groups is run by Google under the Google Privacy Policy.

8. Children's privacy

Lockin-Inator is made for people aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it does not collect personal information from children, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age where they live). If you believe a child has somehow sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your choices and deleting your data

  • Turn off the Shield: in your phone's Settings, under Accessibility, switch off Lockin-Inator. The study timer still works without it.
  • Turn off Do Not Disturb access: in your phone's Settings, under Do Not Disturb access (or Special app access).
  • Cancel your subscription: in the Google Play Store, under Payments & subscriptions.
  • Remove everything: uninstall the app. This deletes everything it saved on your phone.

Because we don't hold any of your data, there is nothing stored with us to request or delete, but you are always welcome to contact us with questions.

10. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, privacy laws such as Canada's PIPEDA, the EU and UK GDPR, or California's CCPA may give you rights to access, correct or delete personal information that a company holds about you, and to know whether it is sold or shared. We do not collect, sell or share personal information, so we hold none about you. If you have a question or want to exercise a right, email us and we will reply within 30 days.

11. Security

The app's settings stay in its private storage on your phone, protected by your phone's own security. Since we don't collect or send your information, it isn't stored on any of our servers.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change what the app does with information, we will update this policy before the change is released, explain exactly what is used and why, and change the "Last updated" date above. Where the law requires it, we will ask for your consent first.
