# Credits

What the NiBi Games website is made of, and under which licences.

## Code

- **three.js** r170 (`assets/3d/three.module.min.js`), the 3D library behind the live sea. MIT licence, Copyright 2010-2024 three.js authors. https://threejs.org

## Fonts

All self-hosted from `assets/fonts/`, cut down to Latin. Each licence text sits next to its font.

- **Sniglet** (headings), by Haley Fiege, Brenda Gallo and Pablo Impallari. SIL Open Font License 1.1.
- **Nunito** (body text), by the Nunito Project Authors. SIL Open Font License 1.1.
- **Archivo** (the Señor Orange card and the beta section), by the Archivo Project Authors (Omnibus-Type). SIL Open Font License 1.1.
- **TeX Gyre Bonum** (the Pond Reels title), by the GUST e-foundry. GUST Font License.

## 3D models, pictures and textures

- **Barnaby Bramble**, his rowboat, **Old Mossback** (with his colour map) and Señor Orange's beach bar are NiBi Games' own, exported from **Pond Reels** with the private tools in the game's repository (`tools/website_export`). Only the shapes and colours are here, none of the game's code.
- The island textures (sand, grass, granite, leaves, bark, palm fronds, thatch, planks) are generated by `tools/gen-textures.py`. NiBi Games' own.
- The still pictures in `assets/stills/` are renders of the site's own 3D scene (`tools/render-stills.py`).
- The NiBi Games logo, the game icons and the screenshots are NiBi Games' own. The Señor Orange lockup is from the NiBi x Señor Orange game kit.
